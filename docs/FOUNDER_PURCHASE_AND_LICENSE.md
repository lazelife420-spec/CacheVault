# Cache Vault Founder Edition — Purchase & License

## What you get

**Cache Vault Free** remains fully usable: capture, search, favorites, copy back out, and local-only storage.

**Cache Vault Founder Edition** ($19 lifetime, early price) unlocks advanced power workflows:

- Proof-pack and ZIP exports
- HTML bundles and editable copy workflows
- Vault Macros and custom Safes
- Advanced review filters

No cloud sync. No accounts. No telemetry.

## How to buy

Purchase is handled externally (manual delivery for MVP):

1. Pay via the configured purchase link when available.
2. Receive your signed `license.json` by email or download.
3. Import the license in the app.

If no purchase URL is configured in your build, contact the seller directly.

## Install your license

1. Open Cache Vault.
2. Sidebar → **Founder**.
3. Choose **Enter License** (paste JSON) or **Import License File**.
4. Restart the app if prompted.

License file location after install:

```text
%LOCALAPPDATA%\CacheVault\license.json
```
